Privacy Policy

NobergCards — Card Inventory Database Sync

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

1. Overview

NobergCards is a trading card inventory and listing management application. The application is used to scan trading cards, organize inventory information, create listing information, track sales, and synchronize information with connected services.

2. Information We Access

When the account owner connects an eBay account to NobergCards, the application may access information made available through eBay's APIs that is authorized by the account owner.

This may include information related to eBay listings and orders, such as order identifiers, item information, quantities, transaction information, and other information made available by eBay through the authorized APIs.

NobergCards may also process information entered by the account owner or generated by the application, including card names, set information, card numbers, card attributes, listing prices, sale information, and inventory identifiers.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through eBay and other connected services is used to operate NobergCards, including inventory management, listing management, order synchronization, sales tracking, and related application functions.

NobergCards does not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Google Sheets

NobergCards may use Google Sheets as its inventory database. Information processed by the application may be stored in the Google Sheets spreadsheet associated with the application.

Access to the spreadsheet is restricted through the Google account and service credentials configured by the application owner.

5. eBay Information

NobergCards uses eBay APIs only after the account owner authorizes the application through eBay's authentication and consent process.

NobergCards does not collect or store eBay passwords.

eBay information is used only for the functionality provided by NobergCards and authorized by the account owner.

6. Authentication and Security

eBay API credentials and authentication tokens are intended to be stored using secure application configuration rather than being displayed publicly in the application's source code.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application credentials and information handled by the application. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as it is necessary to operate NobergCards, maintain inventory and sales records, or provide the functions requested by the account owner.

Personal information received from eBay will be deleted when it is no longer necessary for the legitimate purpose for which it was processed, when deletion is otherwise required by applicable law or policy, or when requested where applicable.

8. Third-Party Services

NobergCards may use third-party services including eBay, Google Sheets, Google Gemini, and hosting providers to provide application functionality.

These third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies governing their services.

9. eBay Marketplace Account Deletion Notifications

NobergCards maintains an endpoint for receiving eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications as required by eBay's developer requirements.

The endpoint is used to receive and acknowledge notifications from eBay regarding marketplace account deletion requests.

10. Your Choices

The account owner may disconnect NobergCards from eBay by revoking the application's authorization through eBay.

The account owner may also remove information from the NobergCards inventory database where supported by the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when NobergCards' functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the NobergCards application, please contact the application owner using the contact information associated with NobergCards.
